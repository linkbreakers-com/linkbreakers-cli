## linkbreakers custom-domains delete

Delete a custom domain

### Synopsis

Removes a custom domain from the workspace, and deletes every link on it with
their analytics (see linkCount on the domain). This operation cannot be undone.

Requires the `domains:write` scope.

API: DELETE /v1/custom-domains/{id}
