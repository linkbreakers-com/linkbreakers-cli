## linkbreakers google-analytics-integrations update

Connect Google Analytics

### Synopsis

Connect a Google Analytics 4 property, or change its settings. Every person's visit, form submission and piece of visitor data captured is then sent to it through the Measurement Protocol, without personal data. With visitorAttributesEnabled on (the default), form answers and custom visitor attributes go with each event as lb_<key> parameters, numbers as numbers; visitorAttributeKeys limits them to chosen keys.

Requires the `integrations:write` scope.

API: PUT /v1/integrations/google-analytics

```
linkbreakers google-analytics-integrations update [flags]
```
