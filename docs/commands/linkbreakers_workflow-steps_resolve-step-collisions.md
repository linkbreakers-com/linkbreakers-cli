## linkbreakers workflow-steps resolve-step-collisions

Resolve step collisions

### Synopsis

Pushes overlapping steps away from a given anchor step using iterative
radial repulsion. The anchor step stays fixed. Use after adding or moving
individual steps to preserve the existing layout.

Requires the `workflows:write` scope.

API: POST /v1/links/{linkId}/workflow-steps/{stepId}/resolve-collisions
