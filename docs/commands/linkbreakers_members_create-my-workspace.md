## linkbreakers members create-my-workspace

Create workspace

### Synopsis

Create another workspace for the signed-in person, such as one per client or brand, and issue an access token for it. The new workspace starts on the Free plan and is billed on its own plan. A person may found a limited number of Free workspaces: past that the call answers FREE_WORKSPACE_LIMIT_REACHED, with the limit in its metadata, until one of them is on a paid plan. Workspaces the person was invited to do not count. Needs a dashboard session or a full-access token of a member with an email; a workspace token or a token limited to scopes is refused.

Requires the `members:write` scope.

API: POST /v1/myself/workspaces

```
linkbreakers members create-my-workspace [flags]
```
