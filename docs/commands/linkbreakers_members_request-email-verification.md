## linkbreakers members request-email-verification

Resend a member's verification email

### Synopsis

Sends the verification email again to a member of the workspace whose email address is not verified yet. Pass the member's email address.

A free workspace with no verified address is paused 7 days after sign-up: its calls answer `WORKSPACE_UNVERIFIED` until a member opens the link in that email. Verifying one member's address, usually the owner's, lifts the pause for the whole workspace. This operation keeps working while the workspace is paused.

The address must belong to a member of the workspace. One email per address per minute, and 5 per day.

Requires the `members:write` scope.

API: POST /v1/members/email-verification

```
linkbreakers members request-email-verification [flags]
```
