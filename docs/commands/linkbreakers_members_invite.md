## linkbreakers members invite

Invite a new member

### Synopsis

Create a pending member and dispatch an invitation email. A person who already has a Linkbreakers account is added to the workspace right away and told by email; inviting someone already in the workspace fails with MEMBER_ALREADY_IN_WORKSPACE.

Requires the `members:write` scope.

API: POST /v1/members/invite

```
linkbreakers members invite [flags]
```
